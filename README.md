## Features

- **Disk Imaging**: Create complete images of your hard drives or partitions to ensure you can restore your system to a previous state in case of failure. Disk imaging captures everything on your disk, including the operating system, applications, and settings, making recovery straightforward.

- **File Backup**: Effortlessly back up specific files and folders on your system to protect against accidental deletion or corruption. This feature is useful for users who may not need a full disk image and prefer to back up only essential files, such as documents, photos, and videos.

- **System Clone**: Migrate your entire operating system to another hard drive with ease, allowing for hardware upgrades or replacements without data loss. This cloning feature is invaluable when upgrading to a new SSD or HDD, ensuring all your settings and software remain intact.

- **Scheduled Backups**: Automate the backup process by scheduling regular backups, ensuring your data is always up to date without manual effort. Users can choose daily, weekly, or monthly schedules based on their needs.

- **Partition Management**: Manage your disk partitions directly, including options to resize, merge, or split partitions as needed. This feature is particularly beneficial for users who wish to optimize disk space and improve system performance.

- **Incremental Backups**: Save storage space and time by only backing up changes made since the last backup, optimizing the backup process. This method reduces the amount of data transferred and speeds up backup operations.

- **Restoration Options**: Recover your files and system easily with multiple restoration options, whether from a local drive or an external backup. Aomei Backupper allows you to restore files to their original location or a new one.

- **User-Friendly Interface**: Navigate the software with a straightforward and intuitive interface designed for users at all technical levels. The layout is designed to guide you through the backup and restore processes without confusion.

- **Bootable Media Creation**: Create bootable USB drives or CDs for recovery in case your system fails to boot. This feature is essential for users whose operating systems have become unresponsive.

- **File Synchronization**: Keep files in sync between locations, ensuring that your most important data is always current. This feature can be particularly useful for users who work on multiple devices or need to share files across different platforms.
